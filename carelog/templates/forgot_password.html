CareMin does not confirm whether an email address has an account.

Trouble signing in

Forgot your password?

CareMin does not send password-reset emails yet.

Provider account

Contact your organisation administrator. They can set a temporary password from the Users page. You will replace it before entering the workspace.

Organisation administrator

Contact CareMin support so your identity and authority can be verified before access is changed.

Lost authenticator

Use one of your recovery codes. If none remain, ask your organisation administrator to reset two-step verification.
