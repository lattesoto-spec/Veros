How it works

First upload of a format is analyzed; repeats are instant

What happens to your file

1. The file's structure (headers + a few sample rows) is analyzed to build a column mapping — the bulk of the data never leaves the server.

2. The mapping is validated against the whole file, then stored against the format's fingerprint.

3. Future uploads with the same structure reuse the stored mapping — instant, no AI cost.
